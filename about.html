A bit about me

I’m Emily, a recent graduate from Simon Fraser University with a BSc in Interactive Arts and Technology. My work has covered web and mobile applications, interactive visualizations, AI projects, and computer-vision systems.

I tend to be most interested in projects where there isn’t one obvious answer. I like figuring out how an interface should work, how information should be organized, and how the technical side can support the experience rather than getting in the way of it. A lot of my university work involved learning by building. That meant trying things, running into problems, changing the approach, and figuring out why something wasn’t working. That process is still a big part of how I approach projects.
