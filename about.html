A bit about me

I recently graduated from Simon Fraser University with a BSc in Interactive Arts and Technology. My work has covered web and mobile applications, interactive visualizations, AI projects, and computer-vision systems.

I enjoy working across design and development: figuring out how something should look and feel, then getting into the details of making it work. I’m curious about new tools and technologies, but I’m most interested in what they can help people do.
